A Dynamic Congress Leader Dedicated to the People

A dedicated and proactive leader of the Indian National Congress deeply aligned with the values of secularism, social justice, and grassroots empowerment. Driven by the sacred resolution of “ಜನಸೇವೆಯೇ ಸಂಕಲ್ಪ | ನಮ್ಮ ಜನರೇ ನಮ್ಮ ಶಕ್ತಿ” (Service to the People is our Resolution | Our People are our Strength), I am committed to prioritizing public welfare, championing youth opportunities, and supporting our hardworking farming community across Karnataka.

“Harish Nagaraju”

Passionate about grassroots progress, public grievance redressal, and active political dialogue. Dedicated to translating people's voices into meaningful developmental action.

Citizen Grievance Cell
01Extensive Grassroots Engagement

Actively working with diverse community, social, and welfare organizations across Karnataka to champion inclusive public development and people-first governance.

02Indian National Congress Cadre Building

Dedicated to strengthening Congress party units, instilling secular ideals, and building energized youth cadres at the booth and mandal levels.

03Janaseveye Sankalpa Outreach

Founded on the guiding ethos “ಜನಸೇವೆಯೇ ಸಂಕಲ್ಪ | ನಮ್ಮ ಜನರೇ ನಮ್ಮ ಶಕ್ತಿ”, conducting regular public listening forums and addressing citizen grievances.

04Youth Leadership & Employment Guidance

Conducting youth conclaves and mentorship camps to guide educated young women and men toward career and civic opportunities.

05Free Health & Medical Checkup Camps (Re-Live Foundation)

Organizing monthly community health camps (4ನೇ ಮಾಸಿಕ ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ಶಿಬಿರ), blood donation drives, free medicine distribution, and eye screening programs in underserved areas.

06Local Trade & MSME Support

Advocating for local merchants, self-employed artisans, and small business owners to ensure fair economic growth and credit accessibility.

07Humanitarian Relief & Emergency Support

Providing rapid humanitarian assistance, food relief, and essential supplies to affected families during climate distress and emergency situations.

08Social Justice & Constitutional Harmony

Upholding Baba Saheb Ambedkar’s constitutional values, uniting diverse sections of society in brotherly solidarity and mutual respect.

09Farmer Support & Krishi Bandhu

Standing firmly with farmers to secure fair Minimum Support Prices (MSP), timely fertilizer distribution, and irrigation improvements.

10Active Digital & Ground Presence

Directly accessible to the public 24/7 across Instagram (@harish_nagaraju_official), Facebook, YouTube (@harishnagarajul), and Twitter/X (@nagaraju_harish).
